Being a Compendium of

Grimoire of Strength

Custom Strength & Mobility Plan

☽ ✦ ☾
Frequency: 2x/week (30 min) + optional 3rd short session (10–15 min)
Variety: alternate Week A and Week B each week so the exercises stay fresh — both keep the same structure and hernia-safe rules
Complements: running, hiking, spin, yoga
Goals: mobility, flexibility, strength/muscle, bone density
Hernia-safe: no heavy deadlifts/squats, no full sit-ups, no pull-ups, no breath-holding under load
A Word of Caution — before beginning this rite, consult a healer (physician or physical therapist) familiar with your hernia. Exhale on exertion; never hold your breath or bear down hard (no Valsalva). "Light–moderate" weight = a load you could do 3–4 more reps with in reserve. Stop any exercise that causes bulging, pulling, or pressure at the hernia site.

First Working — Week A

Waxing Moon · Strength & Bone Loading (Dumbbells + Rack) · ~28 min

✦ Rite of Waking (Warm-up, 4 min)

  • Arm circles — 30 sec each direction
  • Leg swings (front/back + side/side) — 10 each leg
  • Cat-Cow — 8 reps
  • Bodyweight glute bridge — 10 reps

✦ The Working Itself (~20 min)

2–3 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 60–90 sec between rounds (do 2 rounds if you're short on time, to stay within 30 min total)

ExerciseReps / TimeNotes
Rack-supported split squat or step-up8/legUse rack rail for balance, not heavy load
Dumbbell Romanian deadlift10Light-moderate weight, soft knees, hinge from hips
Standing dumbbell overhead press8–10Builds shoulder/upper-body bone density
Barbell bench press (using the power rack)8–10Set safety pins just below chest height; lying position is low-strain for the hernia, keep feet flat, don't arch hard
Single-arm dumbbell row (bench or rack-supported)10/armKeep spine neutral, no twisting
Suitcase hold30–40 sec (or 3x/side if alternating one arm)Stand tall holding a heavy dumbbell at your side, shoulders level, ribs stacked over hips — resists you leaning sideways. No walking space needed.

✦ Rite of Closing (Cool-down, 5 min)

  • Standing quad stretch — 30 sec/side
  • Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 30 sec/side
  • Doorway chest/shoulder stretch — 30 sec
  • Child's pose — 45–60 sec

Second Working — Week A

Waxing Moon · Core Stability, Mobility & Flexibility (Mat + Band) · ~26 min

✦ Rite of Waking (Warm-up, 3 min)

  • Cat-Cow — 8 reps
  • Thread the needle — 6/side
  • Standing hip circles — 8/side

✦ Core & Stability Circuit (~13 min)

2–3 rounds

ExerciseReps / TimeNotes
Bird dog10/sideSlow, controlled, no low-back arch
Dead bug10/sideLow back stays flat on mat
Side plank (knees down if needed)20–30 sec/sideNo breath-holding
Band Pallof press10/sideAnti-rotation, gentle core engagement. Mnemonic: "Press and resist" — anchor band at chest height, press straight out and hold, then return; the whole point is *not* letting your torso twist toward the anchor.
Glute bridge (or banded hip thrust)12Squeeze glutes, not lower back

✦ Mobility Flow (5 min)

  • 90/90 hip switches — 6/side (sit with front leg bent 90° in front, back leg bent 90° to the side, rotate knees side to side keeping chest tall)
  • Thoracic rotations (quadruped) — 8/side (hands and knees, one hand behind head, rotate elbow up toward ceiling then down under torso)
  • World's Greatest Stretch — 5/side (from a lunge, drop back hand to floor, then rotate that same arm up toward the ceiling)

✦ Rite of Closing (Cool-down, 5 min)

  • Figure-4 stretch (lying) — 30 sec/side
  • Gentle supine spinal twist — 30 sec/side
  • Pigeon pose or seated glute stretch — 30 sec/side
  • Deep breathing in child's pose — 45 sec

  • Seated gentle spinal twist — 30 sec/side
  • Legs-up-the-wall — 90 sec (great for recovery and circulation)

The Quick Charm

Mobility & Flexibility Reset · 10–15 min

Use this on a rest day, or split off from Workout 2 if you want a shorter, more frequent mobility habit.

  • Cat-Cow — 10 reps
  • World's Greatest Stretch — 5/side
  • 90/90 hip switches — 8/side
  • Thoracic rotations — 8/side
  • Standing forward fold (soft knees, don't force it) — 30 sec
  • Pigeon pose — 45 sec/side
  • Box breathing or slow nasal breathing — 1 min

✦ On the Growth of Power ✦

  • Add weight only when you can complete all sets with good form and no strain at the hernia site.
  • Every 3–4 weeks, add 1 rep per set or a small weight increase (2.5–5 lbs) rather than jumping sets/reps.
  • The holds, presses, and deadlifts are your main bone-density drivers here — prioritize consistency over heavy load.
  • If a movement ever feels wrong for your hernia, swap it for a bodyweight or banded version rather than pushing through.

# Custom Strength & Mobility Plan

**Frequency:** 2x/week (30 min) + optional 3rd short session (10–15 min)
**Variety:** alternate Week A and Week B each week so the exercises stay fresh — both keep the same structure and core-safe rules
**Designed to complement:** running, hiking, spin, yoga
**Goals:** mobility, flexibility, strength/muscle, bone density
**Core-safe:** no heavy deadlifts/squats, no full sit-ups, no pull-ups, no breath-holding under load

**General rules:**
- Exhale on exertion; never hold your breath or bear down hard (no Valsalva).
- "Light–moderate" weight = a load you could do 3–4 more reps with in reserve.
- Stop any exercise that causes bulging, pulling, or pressure in the core or abdominal wall.

---

## Workout 1 · Week A — Strength & Bone Loading (Dumbbells + Rack) — ~28 min

### Warm-up (4 min)
- Arm circles — 30 sec each direction
- Leg swings (front/back + side/side) — 10 each leg
- Cat-Cow — 8 reps
- Bodyweight glute bridge — 10 reps

### Main Circuit (~20 min) — 2–3 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 60–90 sec between rounds (do 2 rounds if you're short on time, to stay within 30 min total)
| Exercise | Reps/Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet Squads | 10 |  |
| Romanian deadlift (RDLs ✨) | 10 | soft knees, hinge from hips |
| Standing dumbbell overhead press | 8–10 | Builds shoulder/upper-body bone density |
| Barbell bench press | 8–10 | Set safety pins just below chest height; lying position is low-strain on the core, keep feet flat, don't arch hard |
| Single-arm dumbbell row (bench or rack-supported) | 10/arm | Keep spine neutral, no twisting |

### Cool-down Stretches (5 min)
- Standing quad stretch — 30 sec/side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 30 sec/side
- Doorway chest/shoulder stretch — 30 sec
- Child's pose — 45–60 sec

## Workout 1 · Week B (Alternate) — Strength & Bone Loading (Dumbbells + Rack) — ~28 min
Keeps the deadlift and bench press; swaps the rest for variety.

### Warm-up (4 min)
- Cat-Cow — 8 reps
- Standing hip circles — 8/side
- Arm circles — 30 sec each direction
- Standing marches (knees to hip height) — 10/side

### Main Circuit (~20 min) — 2–3 rounds, minimal rest between exercises, 60–90 sec between rounds
| Exercise | Reps/Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Goblet squat | 10 | controlled depth |
| Romanian deadlift (RDLs ✨) | 10 | soft knees, hinge from hips |
| Single-arm dumbbell overhead press (alternating) | 8/side | Unilateral variety builds shoulder stability useful for hiking/running |
| Barbell bench press | 8–10 | lying position stays low-strain on the core |
| Bent-over double-arm dumbbell row | 10 | Hinge at hips, flat back, squeeze shoulder blades together |

### Cool-down Stretches (5 min)
- Standing quad stretch — 30 sec/side
- Kneeling hip flexor stretch — 30 sec/side
- Doorway chest/shoulder stretch — 30 sec
- Child's pose — 45–60 sec

## Workout 2 · Week A — Core Stability, Mobility & Flexibility (Mat + Band) — ~26 min

### Warm-up (3 min)
- Cat-Cow — 8 reps
- Thread the needle — 6/side
- Standing hip circles — 8/side

### Core & Stability Circuit (~13 min) — 2–3 rounds
| Exercise | Reps/Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Bird dog | 10/side | Slow, controlled, no low-back arch |
| Dead bug | 10/side | Low back stays flat on mat |
| Side plank (knees down if needed) | 20–30 sec/side | No breath-holding |
| Band Pallof press | 10/side | Anti-rotation, gentle core engagement. Mnemonic: "Press and resist" — anchor band at chest height, press straight out and hold, then return; the whole point is *not* letting your torso twist toward the anchor. |
| Glute bridge (or banded hip thrust) | 12 | Squeeze glutes, not lower back |

### Mobility Flow (5 min)
- 90/90 hip switches — 6/side (sit with front leg bent 90° in front, back leg bent 90° to the side, rotate knees side to side keeping chest tall)
- Thoracic rotations (quadruped) — 8/side (hands and knees, one hand behind head, rotate elbow up toward ceiling then down under torso)
- World's Greatest Stretch — 5/side (from a lunge, drop back hand to floor, then rotate that same arm up toward the ceiling)

### Cool-down Stretches (5 min)
- Figure-4 stretch (lying) — 30 sec/side
- Gentle supine spinal twist — 30 sec/side
- Pigeon pose or seated glute stretch — 30 sec/side
- Deep breathing in child's pose — 45 sec

## Workout 2 · Week B (Alternate) — Core Stability, Mobility & Flexibility (Mat + Band) — ~26 min

### Warm-up (3 min)
- Thread the needle — 6/side
- Cat-Cow — 8 reps
- Standing marches with arm reach — 10/side

### Core & Stability Circuit (~13 min) — 2–3 rounds
| Exercise | Reps/Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Half-kneeling Pallof press | 10/side | Same "press and resist" cue as the standing version, just from a half-kneeling position — challenges hip and core stability together |
| Bear crawl hold | 20–30 sec | Hands and toes on the mat, knees hovering just off the floor, flat back — no breath-holding |
| Forearm plank with shoulder taps | 10 taps/side | Feet a bit wider for stability; keep hips still as you tap |
| Single-leg glute bridge | 8–10/side | Keep hips level, don't let them dip |
| Standing marches | Up to 50 | Light weights held above head |

### Mobility Flow (5 min)
- Windmill stretch — 6/side (stand wide, hinge slightly forward, rotate reaching one hand to the sky while the other reaches down)
- Standing figure-4 to hip hinge — 5/side (cross ankle over opposite knee, hinge forward gently, stand back up)
- Cobra pose (gentle) — 3 breaths, held lightly

### Cool-down Stretches (5 min)
- Band-assisted supine hamstring stretch — 30 sec/side
- Seated gentle spinal twist — 30 sec/side
- Legs-up-the-wall — 90 sec (great for recovery and circulation)
- Deep breathing in child's pose — 45 sec

## Optional Session 3 — Mobility & Flexibility Reset (10–15 min)
Use this on a rest day, or split off from Workout 2 if you want a shorter, more frequent mobility habit.

- Cat-Cow — 10 reps
- World's Greatest Stretch — 5/side
- 90/90 hip switches — 8/side
- Thoracic rotations — 8/side
- Standing forward fold (soft knees, don't force it) — 30 sec
- Pigeon pose — 45 sec/side
- Box breathing or slow nasal breathing — 1 min

---

## Progression Notes
- Add weight only when you can complete all sets with good form and no strain in the core.
- Every 3–4 weeks, add 1 rep per set or a small weight increase (2.5–5 lbs) rather than jumping sets/reps.
- The holds, presses, and deadlifts are your main bone-density drivers here — prioritize consistency over heavy load.
- If a movement ever feels wrong for your core or joints, swap it for a bodyweight or banded version rather than pushing through.
